Case study · Real estate · terravionproperties.com · terravionproperties.in

Terravion Properties — land, discovered online.

Plot buying near Hyderabad runs on trust: approvals, boundaries, paperwork. Terravion’s platform puts HMDA-approved projects near Shankarpally online with the details that actually decide a purchase — location, approval status, layout — so site visits start with informed buyers.

01 / 07

The problem

Land is the least digital corner of Indian real estate. Buyers hear about projects through brokers, see approvals late, and burn weekends on site visits that a good webpage would have filtered. Terravion wanted the opposite: publish everything a serious buyer checks, before they call.

02 / 07

Challenges

Trust documentationApproval status (HMDA) and project specifics had to be first-class content, not footnotes — that is the buying decision.
Local searchPlot buyers search by corridor and locality; the platform’s structure and content had to match how people actually look for land.
Lead quality over lead volumeThe goal was fewer, better conversations — pages that pre-answer questions instead of a form that harvests numbers.

03 / 07

Architecture & stack

Front endContent-forward React, fully crawlable
ServicesNode.js
DataPostgreSQL for project and enquiry data, Redis for caching
GatewayNginx
PipelineGitHub Actions — tested and AI-reviewed
terravionproperties.inNext.js and TypeScript, with PostgreSQL and PostGIS for the plot map

A content-forward React web platform on our standard production base: Node.js services, PostgreSQL for project and enquiry data, Redis for caching, Nginx at the gateway, deployed through the same tested, AI-reviewed GitHub Actions pipeline as everything we ship. Project pages are fully crawlable and structured, because in this market a project page that ranks is a sales channel.

04 / 07

Results

  • HMDA-approved projects are published with the details buyers verify first.
  • Discovery-to-enquiry runs online; site visits begin with pre-qualified, informed buyers.
  • The platform is the company’s canonical source of truth for every project it sells.

05 / 07 · Part two

A second site: terravionproperties.in

Terravion’s second site, terravionproperties.in, is built for buyers who research before they visit. As published on 23 Nov 2026, it has:

GIS master planA plot map of the projects (the site calls it a GIS digital twin): walk through the township and filter plots by facing, size and budget before the site visit.
Location pages13 pages on the localities around Shankarpally and west Hyderabad, from Kokapet and Tellapur to Mokila and Chevella.
Guides12 buyer guides, from legal verification and the registration process to loans, construction and tax.
Journal72 articles on the west Hyderabad market and on buying a plot.
ProjectsThree project pages: Sanctuary, Raghunath County and Mansanpally.
Site visits and callbacksA site-visit booking form and a callback request, with WhatsApp and call buttons on every page.
EMI and ROIAn EMI calculator, and an ROI calculator with scenarios.

07 / 07

Lessons learned

In high-trust purchases, content architecture is conversion optimisation. Publishing the approval status and project facts plainly did more for enquiry quality than any funnel trick could. We carry that lesson into every marketplace and platform build.
