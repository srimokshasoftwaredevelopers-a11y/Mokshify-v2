Case study · Product · qflow.in

Qflow — skip the wait.

A queue and token system for any business with a queue: customers take a token on their phone, wait anywhere, and walk in when it is their turn.

Open qflow.in ↗
IndustryProduct
TypeOur product
Live atqflow.in

01 / 04

The problem

Salons, clinics, hospitals, restaurants, temples, banks and government offices all share one problem: people standing in a line, unsure how long it will take.

Paper tokens tell nobody when their turn is coming.

02 / 04

What we built

For customersTake a token from the phone, wait anywhere, and get told when it is your turn.
For businessesA business portal to register the shop and run the queue.
For the platformAn admin portal across every business on Qflow.
AccessRuns in the browser at app.qflow.in — nothing to install.
LanguagesEnglish and Telugu.

03 / 04

The result

As qflow.in states on 23 November 2026: live in the browser, an Android app coming to Google Play, available in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana with Guntur first, and a 60-day free trial for businesses.

Qflow is a product of Sri Moksha Software Developers.
