Case study · Agriculture · ajagritech.com

AJ Agritech: a catalogue farmers can read.

A brand and product website for an agri-inputs company, in Telugu first, so farmers and dealers can find the right product for their crop.

Open ajagritech.com ↗
IndustryAgriculture
TypeClient website
Live atajagritech.com

01 / 05

The problem

An agri-inputs company sells to farmers and dealers across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. Most of them read Telugu first, and most of them are on a phone.

They need to know which product suits which crop and stage, and how to reach the company. An English brochure does not tell them.

02 / 05

What we built

Brand and catalogueA brand and product catalogue site for an agri-inputs company serving Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.
LanguagesTelugu by default, with Hindi and English.
CatalogueProducts grouped by crop and by range: growth promoters, flowering boosters and micronutrients.
Admin dashboardThe company manages its own products from an admin dashboard.
EnquiriesAn enquiry form that delivers straight to the company's mailbox.
Dealers and farmersPages for dealer enquiries, crop tips and a farmer helpline.

03 / 05

The result

The site is live at ajagritech.com, in three languages.
