This website sets no cookies

mokshify.io uses no tracking cookies, no advertising cookies, no analytics cookies, and no functional cookies. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and collects no personal data. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to. That is the honest version of compliance.

What could change

If we ever add a tool that sets cookies, this page will list exactly what it sets and why before it goes live, alongside the privacy policy. Third-party services we link out to (WhatsApp, client sites) set their own cookies under their own policies once you are there.
