02 / 06 · Logo usage

Using the logo

Light surfacesThe full-colour mark and wordmark, without the tagline, 36px tall: the scrolled header and paper pages.
Oxblood surfacesThe M mark only, followed by “Mokshify” set in Switzer in the paper colour: the hero header, menus and footer. The maroon and teal wordmark is unreadable on oxblood.
Tagline“Digital solutions. Limitless impact.” appears under the footer logo only, in the mono label style.
ArtworkNever redrawn, recoloured or altered. Only the background is removed and the file cropped or resized.
Files on the webWebP with a PNG fallback, width and height always set, so nothing shifts while it loads.

03 / 06

Palette

Oxblood 900#2A0A12 - the deepest surface: heroes, menus, footer
Oxblood 700#4A1422 - the brand colour; primary buttons on light
Limestone#E4E2DC - the main light surface
Paper#F6F5F1 - the reading surface for long-form pages
Ink#141012 - text on light
Brass#B08D57 - hairlines, markers and focus rings only; never a fill, never text

04 / 06

Typography

Switzer (headlines and interface, self-hosted variable font), Newsreader (long-form reading), JetBrains Mono (labels, metadata and code). Emphasis comes from size and weight, never from italic accent words.

05 / 06

The two rules

  1. Do not distort or recolour the mark. Scale it whole, on light or dark grounds.
  2. Do not imply endorsement. Referencing our work truthfully is welcome; suggesting partnership or approval we have not given is not - see the terms.
