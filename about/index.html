Company · About

The studio behind the film.

Mokshify is an AI-first software engineering studio in Hyderabad, India. The story our homepage tells in 914 frames is the literal one: a founder watching ordinary businesses lose to problems software solves - hospital queues, opaque land deals, financial guesswork - and deciding the answer was a studio that engineers products the way enterprises do, at a pace startups need.

Updated 23 Nov 2026 The scroll film this page describes was retired from the homepage in the November 2026 redesign. The text below is kept as the record of how it was built.

01 / 05

What Mokshify is

A small, senior product engineering team that takes accountability for the whole system: design, code, cloud, deployment and operation. We build AI products, SaaS platforms, mobile apps and enterprise software - and we run them after launch. Four of our builds are live and public; that portfolio policy (only things you can open and use) is the closest thing we have to a slogan.

02 / 05

What Mokshify is not

Not a body shopWe sell outcomes with one accountable team, not resumes by the hour.
Not a demo factoryProduction is the deliverable - staging links, weekly working demos, tested releases.
Not a black boxOur process, technology decisions, security practices and even mistakes are published.

04 / 05

How we think

Three beliefs run through everything: boring technology (spend novelty on the product, not the plumbing - ADR-010); discipline over heroics (tests, dual review and rollback make 2am pages rare); and trust is engineered (published standards beat promised ones). The homepage says it cinematically; the library says it technically; this page says it plainly.
