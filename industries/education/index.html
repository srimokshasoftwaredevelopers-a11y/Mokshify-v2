Industries · Education

Education software that makes quality legible.

We build websites and learning platforms for training institutes, schools and coaching centres.

For an education business, the website is the first lecture: it teaches prospective students what standard to expect. We build platforms where curriculum, outcomes and enrolment are structured data - proven live, in a tier-2 city competing globally.

01 / 06 · The problems

What we build for education

Institute platformscourses, batches, instructors and enrolment in one system.
Course catalogues as datatracks and schedules that are searchable, comparable and findable - because students search for exactly what institutes teach.
Enrolment flowsfrom interested to enrolled-in-a-batch in minimal steps, with human follow-up where it converts.
Mobile-first deliverystudents research between classes and shifts, on modest phones; the platform respects that reality.

03 / 06

What we would build first

A course catalogue with the details that decide enrolment — duration, syllabus, schedule, fees — published as structured data so search engines and parents can read it.

Enrolment that works on a phone, batch and instructor management behind it, and an admin the institute runs itself.
