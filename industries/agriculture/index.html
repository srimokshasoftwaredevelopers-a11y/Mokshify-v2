Industries · Agriculture

Agri-input websites, in the farmer's language.

We build websites for agri-input companies — in Telugu first — so farmers and dealers can find the right product for their crop and reach you in one tap.

Farmers and dealers decide season by season, on a phone, in their own language. An agri-input brand online has one job: show which product suits which crop, and make the next call easy.

01 / 04 · The problems

What we build for agriculture

Product cataloguesproducts by range and by crop, with pack sizes and the crops each one suits. Live example: AJ Agritech.
Local languagesTelugu by default, with Hindi and English, so the catalogue reads the way customers speak.
Dealer and farmer enquiriesdealer enquiry pages, a farmer helpline and an enquiry form that reaches the company's own mailbox.
Admin dashboardthe company adds and edits its own products without waiting on a developer.
