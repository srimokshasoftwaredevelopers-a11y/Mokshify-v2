Industries · Real estate

Property software for high-trust purchases.

We build property websites that show every project and plot clearly and turn visitors into enquiries.

Lead project · Next Edge Realty

Every plot of a layout on one live map, in 2D or 3D, over satellite imagery: buyers search by plot number, filter by size, and see where each plot sits before they visit. Read the Next Edge Realty case study or open nextedgerealty.com ↗.

Nobody buys land on impulse. Real estate software wins by answering the questions buyers verify anyway - approvals, boundaries, paperwork - before the first phone call. We proved it with a live platform, not a theory.

01 / 06 · The problems

What we build for real estate

Discovery platformsprojects, plots and inventory published with the details that decide purchases. Live examples: Next Edge Realty, with a live 2D and 3D plot map on satellite imagery; and Terravion Properties, HMDA-approved plots near Shankarpally, on two sites (terravionproperties.com, and terravionproperties.in with its GIS Master Plan, a plot-by-plot status grid for each project).
Project marketing systemsapproval documentation, layouts and location intelligence as first-class content, structured so search finds it.
Enquiry pipelinesfewer, better conversations: pages that pre-answer questions, funnels that qualify rather than harvest.
Operations toolinginventory, booking states, site-visit scheduling; the back office behind the listings.
